About us

{% endblock %} {% block content %}

We are a community-driven organization dedicated to promoting and supporting local events in Bristol. Our mission is to connect residents with activities that enrich their lives and foster a sense of community.

Founded in 2020, we have been working tirelessly to create a platform where event organizers can share their events and where community members can discover new and exciting opportunities to engage with their city.

Whether you're looking for family-friendly activities, cultural events, or social gatherings, Bristol Community Events is here to help you find it all in one convenient place.

Join us in celebrating the vibrant community of Bristol!

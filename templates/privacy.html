Introduction

Bristol Community Events ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This statement explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have in relation to your data when you use this website.

Data we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, password (stored securely).
  • Contact form data: name, email and message when you contact us via the contact form.
  • Usage data: pages visited and basic analytics to help improve the site (may be aggregated or pseudonymised).

How we use your data

  • To provide and maintain your account and the services on the site.
  • To respond to enquiries submitted via the contact form.
  • To improve and personalise the site, and monitor usage.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

Legal basis for processing

We rely on one or more of the following legal bases to process personal data: your consent (e.g., contact form or sign-up consent), performance of a contract (account management), and our legitimate interests (site maintenance, fraud prevention, analytics).

Cookies

We do not use either first-party or third-party cookies on this website.

Data security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a security incident affecting your personal data occurs, we will take appropriate steps in line with applicable law.

Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights or request information about the personal data we hold about you, contact us via the contact page linked below.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us.

Changes to this statement

We may update this privacy statement from time to time. We will post the updated statement on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

For questions or to exercise your rights, please use our contact form: Contact us.
